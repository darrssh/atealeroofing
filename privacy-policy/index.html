Privacy Policy

What customers say — how we handle your data. Same policy as the original site, restyled.

Who we are

Our website address is: https://www.atealeroofing.co.uk. Contact: sales@atealeroofing.co.uk • 0161 434 1864 • 07813 113362.

Comments

When visitors leave comments we collect data shown in the comments form, plus IP address and browser user agent to help spam detection. An anonymized hash of your email may go to Gravatar (see https://automattic.com/privacy/). After approval, your profile picture is public in the comment context.

Media

If you upload images, avoid embedded location data (EXIF GPS). Visitors can download/extract location data from site images.

Cookies

Comment convenience cookies last 1 year. Login-page temporary cookie (no personal data) expires on browser close. Login cookies 2 days, screen-options 1 year, “Remember Me” 2 weeks (removed on logout). Article-edit cookie (post ID only) expires after 1 day.

Embedded content from other websites

Articles may include embedded content (videos, images, articles). It behaves as if you visited that site — they may collect data, use cookies, embed third-party tracking, and monitor interaction (including if logged in there).

Who we share your data with

If you request a password reset, your IP is included in the reset email.

How long we retain your data

Comments + metadata retained indefinitely to auto-approve follow-ups. Registered users' profile info stored as provided; users can see/edit/delete (except username); admins can see/edit.

What rights you have over your data

With an account or comments, you can request an export of personal data we hold, or request erasure (except data kept for admin/legal/security). Email sales@atealeroofing.co.uk.

Where we send your data

Visitor comments may be checked through an automated spam detection service.
